FAQ

Questions before you start?

Does this guarantee I will make money?

No. Product research can reduce wasted time, but no product is guaranteed to be profitable. Always verify current fees, demand, competition, restrictions and supplier terms yourself.

Do you buy products for me?

No. The service is designed to provide research and decision support. You choose whether a product and supplier are right for your business.

Can I use this if I'm new to Amazon?

Yes. The starter process is intentionally simple. You should still learn Amazon's current seller requirements and policies before purchasing inventory.

How do I get paid?

This starter website is the front end. To accept real payments, connect the purchase buttons to a payment processor such as Stripe or another provider you choose.
