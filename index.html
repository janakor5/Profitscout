FAQ

Questions?

Is ProfitScout free?

Yes. The starter version is designed to be free. Additional premium features may be introduced later.

Does ProfitScout guarantee profit?

No. Product research can help you evaluate an opportunity, but no tool can guarantee that a product will be profitable.

Do I need to be an experienced seller?

No. ProfitScout is being designed to be simple enough for beginners while still being useful for experienced sellers.

What happens next?

We plan to add more research tools, product scoring, saved lists and additional seller features.
